Compact tracker

Choose fixed screen anchors to avoid manual positioning conflicting with bottom placements.

Off by default: Quest Journal hides its mini tracker when Phosphor HUD is active. Turn this on to use Quest Journal's tracker instead. If Phosphor still shows its own tracker, both may appear until you disable Phosphor's tracker in its settings (when supported). This does not modify Phosphor HUD.

Only quests received after the current save's initial quest scan are automatically pinned. Existing quests stay unchanged.

The miniature tracker displays quests selected for tracking in the Pip-Boy instead of your custom pins. This is one-way: no game tracking flags are changed.

Journal layout

Adjust list density and column widths independently of journal appearance and tracker settings.

Controls

The journal and tracker have independent shortcuts. Escape returns from settings or closes the journal.

Use the D-pad or left stick to move the selection. Press A to choose and B to go back. The right stick still moves the cursor. While the Journal is open, these menu controls take priority over custom actions assigned to A, B, or the D-pad.

The K2040 Mods MCM Hub is active. Changes here and in MCM share the same assignments. Unbound disables an MCM shortcut. Ctrl, Shift and Alt combinations can be assigned; game-reserved keys may also trigger vanilla actions. F9 and plain J are reserved for game actions.
